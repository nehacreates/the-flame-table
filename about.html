The Origin Tale

Born From Smoke & Ember

The Flame Table began with a single wood-fired hearth, an unyielding passion for ancient culinary traditions, and a vision to elevate smoke into a premier ingredient. Founded in 2016, our sanctuary has evolved into a temple of high-heat gastronomy.

Every dawn brings fresh local produce from artisan growers, while our master pitmasters kindle native oak logs hours before the first guest crosses our threshold. There are no shortcuts here — only patience, precision, and profound respect for raw ingredients.

Julian Ember Founder & Executive Chef

Behind The Scenes

The Culinary Atelier

Explore the three pillars that govern every dish leaving our open hearth.

Aged White Oak & Ironbark

We exclusively burn seasoned hardwood sourced from sustainable forestry programs. White oak burns hotter and cleaner, imparting a sweet, aromatic smoke profile that gently caramelizes proteins without overpowering their natural essence.

  • Zero fossil fuels or gas assist
  • Consistent 800°F radiant bed

Locally Foraged Botanicals

Our culinary team collaborates daily with regional foragers and organic farmers to source wild herbs, rare forest mushrooms, and heirloom roots that define the seasonal cadence of our menu.

  • 100% organic farm partnerships
  • Harvested within 50 miles daily

Himalayan Salt Dry-Aging Chamber

Prime cuts of Australian Wagyu and grass-fed beef rest for up to 60 days in our custom Himalayan salt-lined aging room. Controlled humidity and airflow concentrate the rich, buttery flavor profiles.

  • Up to 60-day maturation process
  • Natural moisture extraction

Master of the Hearth

Meet Executive Chef Julian

“Fire is the oldest cooking medium known to humanity, yet it remains the most misunderstood. When you master its temperament, food ceases to be sustenance and becomes art.”

With over two decades spent inside Michelin-starred wood-fire kitchens across Europe and Australia, Chef Julian Ember brings an intuitive mastery to The Flame Table. His philosophy bridges rustic primitive technique with modern refined plating.
